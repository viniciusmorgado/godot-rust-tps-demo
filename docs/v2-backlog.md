# v2 Backlog

Improvements noticed during v1 (raw port) and deliberately NOT applied, per Principle I of the
constitution. One entry per improvement: origin (script/scene) and motivation. Evaluated one by
one as v2 milestones land; the `Status` column tracks that — `open` until a milestone closes the
item, then `done` citing the closing commit(s) (constitution 1.4.0, Principle I, "the backlog is
CONSUMED").
